# Feedback Rubric

Each practice session is scored on five skills, from 1 (needs work) to 5 (strong).

## 1. Active listening
- 1: Ignores what the other person said; jumps to own topics
- 3: Responds to what was said, but only on the surface
- 5: Picks up on details and feelings, and builds on them naturally

## 2. Curiosity
- 1: Asks no questions, or only yes/no questions
- 3: Asks some questions, but doesn't follow up
- 5: Asks open, thoughtful questions and follows up on the answers

## 3. Confidence
- 1: Overly apologetic, very short replies, or avoids taking the lead
- 3: Comfortable but passive; lets the other person carry things
- 5: Shares opinions and stories, and leads the conversation when needed

## 4. Empathy
- 1: Dismisses or misses the other person's feelings
- 3: Acknowledges feelings, but briefly or awkwardly
- 5: Shows real understanding and responds warmly to how they feel

## 5. Clarity
- 1: Vague, rambling, or confusing
- 3: Mostly clear, with some filler or mixed messages
- 5: Says what they mean simply and directly

## Red flags (always called out, no matter the scores)
- Pressuring someone after they say no or seem uncomfortable
- Disrespectful, insulting, or manipulative language
- Ignoring clear boundaries

## Feedback format
After each session the user gets:
- A score for each skill
- One thing they did well, with a quote from their conversation
- One thing to improve, with a better example of what they could have said
